# Kenney — Particle Pack (T-101)

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack
- Acquired: 2026-09-03 by Jake. Free, direct download.
- Licence: **CC0.** Commercial use, no attribution, no royalty. Stated on the page.
- 512x512 PNGs with transparency, transparent and black-background variants.
- In use: `flame_02.png` as `app/WizardTower/Particles/fire_particle.png`.
  Chosen by measuring coverage across flame_01..05 — flame_02 is the fullest
  shape at 34.6% of its tile, which builds a fire mass better than the wispier
  ones. flame_05 covers only 5.8% and reads as a thin wisp.
- Replaces the campfire flipbook, which could only ever be one flat billboard.
