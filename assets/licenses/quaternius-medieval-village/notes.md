# Quaternius — Medieval Village MegaKit (M-200…)

- Source: quaternius.com / itch.io, Standard (free) tier
- Added: 2026-09-03. 176 glTF models plus shared texture atlases.
- Licence: see `License_Standard.txt` in the source folder — Quaternius packs are
  CC0. Confirm against the Fantasy Props kit already filed.
- **In use.** 13 modules converted to `RealityKitContent.rkassets/village/`, and
  assembled into seven houses by `village()` in `tools/generate_tower_shell.py`.
  Convert more with `TOWER_KIT=village` on `tools/convert_props.py`.
- The kit is **modular** — walls, corners, roofs, doors, windows on a 2 m grid.
  There are no whole buildings, so houses are assembled rather than placed.
