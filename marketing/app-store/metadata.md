## Reviewer notes (draft)

> Wizard Tower is an immersive environment app (not a game): a cozy wizard's study the user sits in while using other apps. It uses WeatherKit with the user's location (with permission) to match the window's weather to the real world, plus a time-of-day system and tap interactions (candles, fireplace). No account or setup needed — first launch goes straight to the environment.
